# Superior Chemical Co (SCC) — brand brief

The brief as it stands, for use in copy, design direction and social posts.
Client names, the supplier's name and internal contacts are deliberately left
out: they do not go into a third-party tool.

## Who we are

* SCC sells solvents and services separately. Solvents are the supply side.
  Consultation, R&D support and AI-driven system design are the lead offer.
* Service categories: solvents, consultation, and waste management
  (brokering).
* Full-stack offer: SCC sells the solvent, a sister brand handles R&D and
  consultation, and a sister entity brokers waste. Each page speaks from its
  own role. Do not blur the roles.
* Selling points: one-stop shop, cradle-to-grave service, green and
  eco-friendly, in-house researchers, customers earn credit toward virgin
  solvent when they recycle with SCC or a partner, and our own database and AI.
* SCC never takes title to product. We broker and arrange carriers. Follow DOT
  rules and note labeling and placarding for hazmat shipments.
* We do not offer tolling.

## Voice and tone

* Clean, modern, professional, and confident. Use industry language
  accurately, without jargon for its own sake.
* Lead with the customer's problem or outcome, then the solution.
* Plain sentences. Short paragraphs. No hype, no exclamation points, no emojis.
* Sound like a specialist who knows the chemistry and the compliance, not a
  generic marketer.

## Brand visuals

* Black, white, and rose-gold palette. Minimal layouts with generous
  whitespace.
* Use molecule and structural imagery, such as 2D skeletal drawings and 3D
  molecule viewers, as supporting visuals, not decoration.
* Logo and visuals must look premium and uncluttered.

## Rules

* Never name our solvent supplier in anything public-facing. Refer to it as
  "our supplier" or leave it out.
* Public products must show no vendor or platform branding. The platform is
  presented as GREA Technologies.
* Never claim we hold inventory right now, since we broker. Never imply we take
  title to product.
* Compliance claims must be accurate. Do not state an EPA or hazardous-waste
  status unless it has been confirmed.
* Pricing and payment terms: default is Net 0, and customers apply for Net 30.
  Do not quote prices.
* Offer new customers one free consultation and one free waste audit.

## Product categories (for context only)

Hydrocarbons, alcohols, glycols, oxygenates, and specialty chemicals. Container
sizes range from 55-gallon drums to 28,500-gallon railcars.
